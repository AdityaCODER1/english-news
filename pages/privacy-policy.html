Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy — Newzire

At Newzire, respecting reader privacy is a core priority. This Privacy Policy explains what limited technical information may be handled when you browse our digital news portal and how we safeguard reader preferences.

Browser Preferences & Local Storage

Newzire uses lightweight browser storage (localStorage) exclusively to remember your chosen Light or Dark reading theme so the interface loads cleanly without visual flicker. No personally identifiable information or account credentials are stored in your browser.

Server Log Files & Hosting

Like standard static websites hosted on modern edge networks, routine web server logs may record non-personal technical metadata such as browser type, referring page, and timestamps strictly for reliability, security, and aggregate traffic measurement.

Cookies & Third-Party Advertising Partners

If third-party advertising networks or sponsored partner widgets are enabled in the future, they may use standard cookies to measure delivery and relevance. Readers can manage or opt out of personalized ad cookies through their browser settings or ad-network preference pages at any time.

External Links & Contact

Articles may cite external source links for reader verification. Newzire is not responsible for the privacy practices of external domains. For privacy questions, write to contact@newzire.com.

Editorial Policy

Newzire — Editorial Standards & Fact-Checking Policy

Newzire is committed to responsible, accurate, and high-signal English journalism covering India and the world. Our editorial process is anchored in transparency, source attribution, and strict factual integrity.

Sourcing & 100% Fact Lock

Our newsroom draws on established national and international wire services, reputable newspapers, official government press releases, and verified institutional reports. Names, figures, dates, quotations, and core events are strictly preserved without fabrication or distortion. Unverified rumors and sensational clickbait are excluded from publication.

Editorial Perspective

Newzire reports through an informed, forward-looking, India-First analytical lens—highlighting technological progress, economic resilience, national strategy, and citizen impact—while keeping hard facts completely untouched and clearly distinguishing Opinion and Analysis columns from straight news reporting.

Image & Source Attribution

We respect intellectual property and transparent sourcing. Every published story displays clear photo credit below its lead image—acknowledging wire agencies, Creative Commons creators, or the Newzire News Desk.

Corrections & Updates

When new verified facts emerge or an error is identified, articles are promptly updated. Readers may submit correction notices anytime to contact@newzire.com.
